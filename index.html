AI-Powered Evangadi Forum

A Q&A community where users ask questions, share answers, and interact with each other. I’m proud of connecting the front end and back end, and of exploring how AI can improve the question-and-answer experience.

  • Sign up and log in with JWT authentication and hashed passwords
  • Post, browse, and search questions, with pagination
  • Answers with upvotes and downvotes, plus notifications
  • AI search and similar-question suggestions using Gemini embeddings
  • Answers ranked by AI relevance and votes
  • A relational MySQL database with 10 tables
